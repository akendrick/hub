How to Play

  • Numbers 1–120 fill the grid. Most are hidden — use math to navigate!
  • Click any square. Answer the addition or subtraction question to fire at that coordinate.
  • Blast Radius: Correct answers pulse all 8 neighbours — red means a ship segment is there, blue means water.
  • Sinking a ship flashes the screen red!
  • Adjust visibility, operation type, fleet size and more in Settings.
